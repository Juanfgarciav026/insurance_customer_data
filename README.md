# US Insurance Market & Customer Intelligence

A Power BI portfolio project designed to demonstrate how analytics can transform customer data into actionable business insights within the insurance industry.

---

## Business Objective

The goal of this project was to build an executive dashboard capable of answering three key business questions:

- Which customer segments demonstrate stronger engagement and conversion potential?
- Where are the largest cross-sell opportunities within the portfolio?
- How can market conditions influence strategic decision-making?

The solution was designed from the perspective of a Marketing Analytics and Insights Specialist, bridging the gap between data and business stakeholders.

---

## Dashboard Overview

The dashboard is structured into four analytical layers:

### Executive Overview
Provides leadership with a high-level understanding of:

- Total Customers
- Interested Customers
- Conversion Rate
- Average Annual Premium
- High Opportunity Customers
- High Opportunity Rate

---

### Customer Intelligence

Explores customer behavior through segmentation analysis based on:

- Age Groups
- Gender
- Customer Status
- Vehicle Age
- Premium Tiers

---

### Channel & Cross-Sell Analytics

Identifies growth opportunities through:

- Premium Tier Distribution
- Cross-Sell Opportunity Mapping
- Decomposition Analysis

---

### Market & Economic Insights

Adds external context using macroeconomic indicators:

- U.S. GDP
- Inflation
- Healthcare Expenditure

---

## Key Business Insights

### 1. Acquisition Opportunities

New Prospects exhibited significantly stronger conversion rates than Existing Insurance Holders, suggesting that acquisition initiatives may currently generate greater returns.

### 2. High-Value Segments

High Premium customers demonstrated the strongest response rates, indicating a higher willingness to engage with additional products and services.

### 3. Geographic Concentration

Cross-sell opportunities were concentrated in a limited number of states, suggesting that geographically focused campaigns could maximize marketing efficiency.
